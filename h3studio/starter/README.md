# Tidal Relay

Tidal Relay is an original ten-second animatic for H3 Creative Studio. The opening shot approaches a coastal listening station and its copper orbital instrument. The second shot moves across the receiver in the analog listening room. The third pulls away from the shore.

The three photographic source plates were generated with OpenAI image generation for this example. Their filenames are `headland.jpg`, `receiver.jpg` and `breakwater.jpg`. The matching PNGs are original editable color-grade layers. The opening title uses Barlow Semi Condensed SemiBold, licensed under the SIL Open Font License in [the font directory](../web/fonts/OFL.txt).

The MP4s contain pre-rendered camera moves across those plates at 1280 × 720 and 24 fps. Each includes its section of the original score, *Listening Line*, which combines synthesized drones, damped tones and filtered noise. The M4A contains the continuous stereo score at 48 kHz. The saved film routes audio from that track once. Each shot also stores a separate editable H3 generation recipe and three source-resolution guide revisions.

These assets are bundled with the Studio under the repository license. Opening the example creates an editable local project. Reopening it preserves the user's edits.
